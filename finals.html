About the Puskás Aréna

The Puskás Aréna in Budapest is one of Europe’s most modern and impressive football stadiums. Named after Hungarian legend Ferenc Puskás, the stadium combines modern design with a rich footballing heritage.

As a host venue for the 2026 UEFA Champions League, the stadium continues to stage some of the biggest matches in European football. With a capacity of over 65,000, it provides an incredible atmosphere and has already hosted major finals and international fixtures.

The Puskás Aréna is located in Budapest and is easily accessible through public transport, with strong metro and bus connections linking it to the city centre.

For the 2026 UEFA Champions League, fans are encouraged to arrange accommodation early, as the city is expected to be in high demand during such a major European event.
